## What I made

A two-day programme facilitating educators to build their own making projects —
helping them prototype, troubleshoot and problem-solve their way to something they had
made themselves, rather than something they had been shown.

## What I did myself

I set up the North American programme, which ran from 2016 to 2019, and oversaw its
transition to being run by a colleague.

## What I learned

This is the evidence that I can establish a practice, not only produce within one.
Handing it over intact mattered as much as starting it.

## Where it went

More than 2,000 educators went through it. The UK programme ran from 2014 to 2019, and
North America from 2016 to 2019.
