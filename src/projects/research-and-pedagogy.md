## What I made

An MSc in STEM Education, examining how policy, practice and concepts of play, making
and creativity meet in the classroom.

A dissertation on the affective, embodied and symbolic knowledge learners bring to
bear when debugging physical computing projects — what they feel, what they do with
their hands, and what they reach for as an explanation when something doesn't work.

## What I did myself

I own the pedagogical principles and standards for a global education organisation,
developed alongside research and impact colleagues.

## What I learned

This isn't a parallel academic interest. It's how I know whether something worked.

It also has a fast version, and the fast version is the one I use most: watching three
children for twenty minutes and changing the thing that afternoon.
